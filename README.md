## The story

GROKBUTT got deleted. Now it has to fight its way out of the server and back onto the home screen, through 7 stages and one very angry boss.

GROKBUTT is shy, so it never faces its enemies. It fights with its butt.

## The stages

1. **App-Icon Graveyard:** dead apps, zombie icons, and one tombstone for "My First App (never shipped)".
2. **Spam Folder:** 1,284,551 unread, and all of them want your attention.
3. **Downloads Folder:** everything is stuck at 99%. Dodge the giant loading wheel, the `.crdownload` files that freeze and then lunge, and the download bombs. Kill a bomb before it finishes "installing".
4. **CAPTCHA Maze:** the floor is a picture grid. Tiles that get a blue check mark flip over into holes, so step off them. Watch for CAPTCHA guards and distorted letters that teleport around.
5. **Trash Bin:** the walls close in a little every wave (watch for the caution stripes), the shredders warm up and then shred, and "Empty Trash?" pop-ups target the spot you're standing on.
6. **Notification Storm:** red 99+ badges swarm you and split in two when you pop them. Toast pop-ups slide across the screen, and the lane flashes red first.
7. **Final boss, the CROP TOOL:** a giant crop frame that wants to crop GROKBUTT out of the picture.
   - **Crop rectangle:** stay inside the frame. Everything outside it hurts.
   - **Corner handles** slam down where a red ▼ mark appears.
   - **Scissors** dash along a red dashed line.
   - It **pastes in minions**.
   - It has **3 phases** and gets faster in each one.
   - **Weak spot:** the pink padlock on its back. Hits from behind do double damage, and butt bumps do extra damage too. Beat it and the frame shatters, and you're back on the home screen.

Your HP carries over between stages, and every door heals you a little.

## How to play

**Phone:** drag on the left side of the screen to move. Hold the right side to butt-attack (it aims for you).

**PC:** click the game once first, so your keys go to the game and not to X. Then move with WASD or the arrow keys, aim with the mouse, and attack with a click or Space. P pauses and M mutes. If you click outside the game, it pauses until you click it again.

**Clearing a room:** when the door at the top says **GO**, walk up to it, or click/tap the door. If you just wait, it takes you through by itself after 8 seconds. The door shows the name of the next stage.

## Loot

- **Cursor Sword:** a giant mouse pointer, clenched in the butt.
- **Stapler Gun:** fires 3 staples at once.
- **USB Lance:** a long jab that goes through every enemy in a line. Drops from stage 3 on.
- **Bubble Farts:** big green bubbles that knock enemies back.
- **Peach Pits:** rapid fire.
- **Toot Missile:** homing. Goes *TOOT!!*
- **"Not a Robot" Shield:** a rare drop that blocks 3 hits.
- **Ctrl+Z:** undoes recent damage and gives back the HP you lost over the last few seconds. Drops from stage 3 on.
